# cashtoken-stakeholders

**Summary**: Ecosystem stakeholder responses and public statements of support for the CashTokens (CHIP-2022-02) upgrade, activated in the May 2023 Bitcoin Cash upgrade.

**Sources**: CHIP-2022-02-CashTokens (cashtokens.org/docs/spec/stakeholders.md)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

The CashTokens CHIP (CHIP-2022-02) achieved widespread ecosystem approval through the [Cash Improvement Proposal (CHIP) process](https://blog.bitjson.com/bitcoin-cash-upgrade-2023/#why-chips). The CHIP maintainer (Jason Dreyzehner) actively sought out stakeholders across nodes, wallets, projects, and industry to demonstrate broad support. Stakeholders were asked to approve, disapprove, or abstain (source: CHIP-2022-02-CashTokens stakeholders.md).

## Public Statements

Notable statements submitted in support of CashTokens (source: CHIP-2022-02-CashTokens stakeholders.md):

> Beyond simply being able to represent other assets on Bitcoin Cash, CashTokens also enable decentralized applications on Bitcoin Cash comparable to Ethereum (ETH) contract functionality, while retaining Bitcoin Cash's >1000x efficiency advantage in transaction and block validation.
>
> — **Jason Dreyzehner**, Chaingraph, Libauth, Bitauth IDE

> Not only do these primitives give BCH the capability to have traditional tokens akin to those found on other chains, it also gives BCH incredibly powerful smart-contract capabilities comparable to those available in EVM. And best of all, it does it at effectively zero-cost to scaling and in a way that aligns naturally with BCH's UTXO model.
>
> — **Jim Hamill**, CashTags, Developers.Cash

> I believe CashTokens is a much needed addition to the BCH consensus rules that will be very lightweight and low cost (as in zero cost) for anybody not interesting in using it. ... BCH is already very fast and scalable, and if it also has utxo-based tokens and better smart contracts, there is no reason for us not to get to the moon and beyond!
>
> — **Calin Culianu**, Bitcoin Cash Node, Fulcrum, Electron Cash

> Approved! This CHIP adds valuable and powerful capabilities to Bitcoin Cash. I am particularly pleased that it does so efficiently and without at all harming the monetary properties of the coin.
>
> — **Tom Zander**, Flowee
